# The Full-Stack Metamorphosis

Welcome to my corner of the web. This is the place where I'm documenting my evolution from a seasoned iOS engineer to an AI-assisted full-stack developer.

![Shibili Areekara](/assets/img/profile.jpg){: width="300" height="300" .w-50 .left}
**I'm Shibili, a Senior iOS Engineer by trade, now partnering with AI to explore the vast world of full-stack development, one line of code at a time.**

In the daytime, I architect and build scalable iOS apps 👨‍💻 for major financial institutions, with **8+ years of experience** shipping features to millions. By night, I switch gears—trading some Swift for stacks and teaming up with my AI co-pilot 🤖 to tackle personal projects and document the entire expedition on this blog.

My primary initiative is building a cross-platform **Personal Management System (PMS)**, which is my proving ground for mastering advanced SwiftUI and full-stack integration with Supabase. I've shared the detailed [origin story for this project](/posts/find-your-niche-how-job-insecurity-and-ai-pushed-me-to-start-building/) right here on the blog.

To put my process to the test under extreme pressure, I also recently completed a public **[7-Day React Native Challenge](/posts/7-day-challenge-complete-a-pro-level-react-native-app-in-under-11-hours/)**, building a full crypto tracker app in just under 11 hours.

> This blog is a real-time log of that journey—a blend of **deep-dive technical solutions and the candid, unfiltered process** of learning something new. It's about the 'aha!' moments and the 'why didn't that work?' head-scratchers.

Learning should be a shared adventure.
